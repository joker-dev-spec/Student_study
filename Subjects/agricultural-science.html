🌱 Introduction to Agriculture

Definition

Agriculture is the science and art of producing crops and rearing animals for food, raw materials and income. It is divided into crop production and animal husbandry.

Key Concepts

  • Branches: crop science, animal science, soil science, agricultural economics and extension.
  • Importance: provides food, employment, raw materials for industries and foreign exchange.
  • Systems: subsistence (for family use) and commercial (for sale/profit).
Two Main Branches Agriculture = Crop Production + Animal Husbandry (Livestock)
Diagram Description A diagram with "Agriculture" at the centre, branching to crop production (grains, vegetables) on one side and animal husbandry (poultry, cattle, fish) on the other.
Worked Example State two reasons why agriculture is important to a nation.
1) It provides food for the growing population. 2) It provides employment and raw materials for industries.
Exam Tip Be able to list at least four importance of agriculture and give examples of crops and animals.

🟫 Soil and Soil Formation

Definition

Soil is the loose, weathered upper layer of the earth's crust in which plants grow. It is formed by the breaking down of rocks (weathering) over a long period, mixed with humus and living organisms.

Key Concepts

  • Soil profile: horizons from the surface down — topsoil (A), subsoil (B) and parent rock (C).
  • Soil types: sandy (fast drainage), clayey (water-logged), loamy (best for crops — holds water and air).
  • Soil constituents: mineral particles, humus, water, air and living organisms.
Soil Components Mineral matter + Humus + Water + Air + Organisms
Diagram Description A cross-section of a soil profile showing a dark topsoil layer rich in humus, a lighter subsoil layer below it, and solid parent rock at the bottom.
Worked Example Which soil type is best for growing most crops and why?
Loamy soil — it retains enough water and also allows air to reach plant roots, and it is rich in humus.
Exam Tip Learn the three main soil types and their properties; remember loamy soil is best for agriculture.

🌽 Crop Production

Definition

Crop production is the growing of plants for food and raw materials. It involves land preparation, planting, care (weeding, fertilising, pest control) and harvesting.

Key Concepts

  • Classification: cereals (maize, rice), legumes (beans, groundnut), roots/tubers (cassava, yam), vegetables (tomato, okra).
  • Fertilisers: organic (manure, compost) and inorganic (NPK). They replace nutrients in the soil.
  • Pests and diseases reduce yield; they are controlled by pesticides and good farm practices.
Farming Operations Land clearing → Ploughing → Planting → Weeding → Fertilising → Harvesting → Storage
Diagram Description A maize plant labelled with its parts — roots, stem, leaves, tassel (male flower), cob and silk (female flower).
Worked Example Give two examples of cereal crops and two examples of leguminous crops.
Cereals: maize, rice (also millet, guinea corn). Legumes: beans, groundnut (also cowpea, soya bean).
Exam Tip Learn the classification of crops with two examples each — this is a very common objective question.

🛠️ Farm Tools and Implements

Definition

Farm tools are simple instruments used for farm operations. Implements are larger machines drawn by animals or tractors.

Key Concepts

  • Simple tools: hoe (weeding, ridging), cutlass (clearing), rake (gathering), spade/shovel (digging, lifting), watering can, wheelbarrow.
  • Implements: plough (turns soil), harrow (breaks lumps), ridger, planter.
  • Simple farm machines: tractor, combine harvester, incubator, milking machine.
Tool Care Clean after use · Keep dry · Sharpen cutting edges · Store in a dry shed · Oil metal parts
Diagram Description Labelled drawings of a hoe, cutlass, spade and wheelbarrow, each showing the handle and the working part.
Worked Example Which farm tool is most suitable for transplanting seedlings and why?
A hand trowel (or hoe) — it lifts the seedling with its soil ball carefully with little root damage.
Exam Tip Match each tool to its correct use. Remember the difference between a spade (digging) and a shovel (lifting loose materials).

🐄 Animal Husbandry (Livestock)

Definition

Animal husbandry is the branch of agriculture concerned with the rearing and care of farm animals for food, income and other products.

Key Concepts

  • Farm animals: poultry (fowls), cattle, sheep, goats, pigs, rabbits, fish.
  • Products: meat, eggs, milk, wool, hides and skins.
  • Animal feeds: carbohydrates (energy), proteins (growth), vitamins, minerals and water.
  • Housing: protects animals from weather, predators and disease.
Poultry Terms Cock (male) · Hen (female) · Chick (young) · Incubation ≈ 21 days
Diagram Description A labelled diagram of a deep-litter poultry house showing feeders, drinkers, perches and a brooder for young chicks.
Worked Example How long does it take for a hen's egg to hatch, and what is the process called?
About 21 days; the process is called incubation.
Exam Tip Learn the correct names for male, female and young of common animals, and the products obtained from each.
