🗺️ Map Reading

Definition

A map is a representation of the Earth's surface drawn to scale on a flat surface. Map reading involves interpreting scale, direction, relief, and features.

Key Concepts

  • Scale: ratio of map distance to actual ground distance, e.g. 1:50,000.
  • Contour lines: join points of equal height above sea level.
  • Latitude: distance north/south of the Equator (0°).
  • Longitude: distance east/west of the Prime Meridian (0°).
  • Meridians: lines of longitude; Parallels: lines of latitude.
Scale Calculation Actual distance = Map distance × Scale factor
Worked Example On a map with scale 1:100,000, two towns are 5 cm apart. Find the actual distance.
5 cm × 100,000 = 500,000 cm
= 5,000 m = 5 km
WAEC Tip Closely spaced contour lines indicate a steep slope; widely spaced lines indicate a gentle slope. A scale of 1:50,000 means 1 cm on the map = 50,000 cm (500 m) on the ground.

⛰️ Physical Geography

Definition

Physical geography studies natural features of the Earth: its structure, landforms, rivers, climate and vegetation. The Earth has three main layers: the crust, mantle and core.

Rock Types

  • Igneous: formed from cooled magma/lava (e.g. granite, basalt).
  • Sedimentary: formed from deposited sediments (e.g. limestone, sandstone).
  • Metamorphic: changed by heat/pressure (e.g. marble from limestone).

Weathering and Erosion

  • Weathering: breakdown of rocks in situ (physical, chemical, biological).
  • Erosion: transport of weathered material by water, wind or ice.
  • Deposition: laying down of eroded material (e.g. flood plains).
WAEC Tip The Niger Delta was formed by deposition of sediments. The River Nile is Africa's longest river. The atmosphere's most abundant gas is nitrogen (78%), followed by oxygen (21%).

🌦️ Climatology

Definition

Climate is the average weather condition of a place over a long period (30+ years). Nigeria has tropical climates: equatorial in the south and tropical savanna in the north.

Weather Instruments

  • Barometer: atmospheric pressure
  • Thermometer: temperature
  • Rain gauge: rainfall
  • Anemometer: wind speed
  • Hygrometer: humidity

Types of Rainfall

  • Convectional: intense heating causes rising air (common in Lagos).
  • Relief (orographic): moist air forced over mountains.
  • Frontal: warm and cold air masses meet.
WAEC Tip The troposphere (lowest atmospheric layer) is where all weather occurs. The world's most abundant gas is nitrogen. Convectional rainfall is characterised by heavy afternoon downpours in tropical regions.

🏙️ Human Geography

Definition

Human geography studies people, their activities, and their relationship with the environment — including population, settlement, agriculture, industry and trade.

Population

  • Population density = total population / area (persons per km²).
  • Birth rate and death rate determine population growth.
  • Population pyramid: a wide base indicates a young, fast-growing population.

Nigeria Facts

  • 36 states + Federal Capital Territory (Abuja).
  • Lagos is the most populous city and an economic hub.
  • Crude oil is produced mainly in the Niger Delta region.
  • Hausa-Fulani in the north, Yoruba in the south-west, Igbo in the south-east.
WAEC Tip Density-dependent factors (disease, food supply) depend on population size; density-independent factors (floods, earthquakes) do not. The main occupation in the savanna is farming and livestock rearing; in coastal areas, fishing.
